## Before You Start

In a battle, each Pokémon has up to four moves that it will know;
- a damaging attacking move,
- a protective defensive move,
- an attribute changing status move, and
- a move unique to the Pokémon who uses it.

Read the move descriptions each battle to find the correct combination and win! But be careful, moves may have slightly different effects depending on the battle. Not all battles will require KO'ing your opponent, and some may introduce new moves!
