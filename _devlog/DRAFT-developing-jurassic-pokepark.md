## What the TARC?

Well it's that time of year again, summer rolls round and Team Leader **Archie** announces that it's time for the annual Team Aqua Romhacking Competition, or TARC for short. Each year, the folk over in Team Aqua's Hideout host a contest to make a short, small-scale, themed rom hack, in the spirit of completion and friendly competition. I was hesitant to enter and about the reserve myself to observing from afar, however, when **Bivurnum** asked if I wanted to join his team to help combat the burnout I was feeling, and told me that **Nico** would also be joining, it felt borderline rude to not jump on the opportunity to with the amount of talent between the two.

## Regigigas: A Slow Start

The slight issue was, well, life. And here started the common theme, life will always find a way… *to kick you in the ass*. I was away for the first week of the competition, and I came back to *a lot* of Discord messages of **Nico** and **Biv** discussing the plans for our entry, so subsequently did *a lot* of backreading. When I tried to get started, I realised that I had not done any rom hacking for *a while*. So in order to get back into it, I did the simple task of creating some new animation tables and inserting a large Tyrantrum sprite from the Pokémon Mystery Dungeon Sprite Repository.

And as I did this… **life happened**.

We lost a team member, and all of a sudden had to find a replacement. **Biv**'s shoes are some large ones to fill, so we made the really bold decision… to not find a replacement because we really wanted to handicap ourselves. Okay, not quite, but Nico and I did struggle to find another teammate, but what was the worst that can happen.
